How it's organised

  1. Video — 3 choices, each a segmented control with a one-line "why". Unavailable options greyed (e.g. GPU when no /dev/dri, 1080p when already 1080p).
  2. Size — Quality vs Target switch. Quality = 3 presets; raw QP/CRF + speed hidden under Advanced.
  3. Audio — one toggle, shows exactly which tracks change.
  4. Estimate card — always visible. Instant rough number on every change (bitrate model), then refined by a background sample encode (~30 s–2 min). "Frees now" vs "after seeding" per your hardlink choice.
  5. Footer — one primary action; count in label.

Validation (quality score on samples) isn't a setting — it always runs.
